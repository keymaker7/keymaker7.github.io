## 최근 글

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
    file.link as "제목",
    file.cday as "날짜"
FROM "금융"
WHERE !contains(file.name, "_") AND !contains(file.name, "디스클레이머")
SORT file.ctime DESC
LIMIT 10
```
